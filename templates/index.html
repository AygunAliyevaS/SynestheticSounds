Create Art That Sings

Synesthetica transforms your brush strokes into harmonious sounds. Draw with vibrant colors and hear your artwork come to life in real-time. Unleash your creativity and experience art like never before!

ℹ️

Use the brush tools to draw on the canvas. Select a brush type, adjust the size, and pick a color. Each color corresponds to a musical note, creating a unique sound as you draw. Click "Submit" to generate an audio file from your artwork. Undo or clear the canvas as needed.

Spray Brush

Creates a textured, misty effect with a soft, diffused sound.

Star Brush

Draws star-shaped patterns with a twinkling, harmonic tone.

Cross Brush

Produces cross shapes with a bold, rhythmic sound.

Square Brush

Draws square patterns with a steady, pulsating tone.

Triangle Brush

Creates triangular shapes with a sharp, clear note.

Sawtooth Brush

Generates jagged lines with a rich, buzzy sound.
